How it works

Three steps, then you're talking

STEP 01

Open the chat

Hit the button and the chat opens right here on the page. Nothing to install, no account to create, no card details.

STEP 02

Ask in plain language

Type the way you'd ask a person. You don't need special phrasing or prompt tricks — if it's unclear, it'll ask you what you meant.

STEP 03

Keep going

Push back, ask for a shorter version, ask why. It remembers the conversation, so you can refine an answer instead of starting over.
